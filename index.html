Confirm before sending shows each change for your approval before it’s written to your Carta. Off, a tap sends it — your Carta is still re-checked first. Finish flash pulses this screen green (and buzzes, where the phone supports it) when a session finishes on its own — never when one is stopped early. Cool-down flash pulses it blue once the chamber has cooled to 150 °F. Neither sends anything to your Carta.
