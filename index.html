Live curve
How the test works
A real curve changes the temperature while your Carta is running. The test starts a session at your Peak (510°F or more), sends ONE change 6 s after Ready (down to 480°F, the lowest allowed, so it never gets hotter than you chose), then checks your Carta’s last reading at the end of the session. Its reading follows the change either way, so that last reading is what tells whether the heat did. Cancel always works.
